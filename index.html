🛡️ Flood Safety Guide

Before a flood

  • Prepare drinking water, dry food, medicines, a torch and batteries.
  • Keep important documents in a waterproof bag.
  • Know your local safe shelter and evacuation route.

During a flood

  • Move to higher ground when safe and follow official instructions.
  • Stay away from floodwater, flooded roads and electric wires.
  • Never walk or drive through moving floodwater.

After a flood

  • Avoid unsafe water and damaged buildings or electrical equipment.
  • Drink safe water and follow local health advice.
  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
